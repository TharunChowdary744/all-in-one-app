OmniKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

OmniKit ("the app") is a collection of file, text and developer tools for the web, Android and iOS. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data we collect

None. OmniKit has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your files, text or any personal information to us or to any third party.

On-device processing

Every conversion (images, PDFs, documents, text, hashes, passwords, QR codes and so on) runs entirely on your device. Files you open are read only to perform the conversion you request. Results are shared only when you choose to save or share them through your device's share sheet.

Permissions

  • Photos / media: used only when you choose images to convert, resize or combine into a PDF.
  • Files: used only when you choose a file to hash or import (for example a CSV or JSON file).

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Local storage

Your favorite tools, recently used tools and usage counts are stored locally on your device so the dashboard can show them. You can clear this history from the app's About screen, or remove it by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
